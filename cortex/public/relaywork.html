Questions, answered

What exactly do you build?

Working automations against the tools you already use: inbox triage, quoting, reporting, data entry, follow-ups and internal handoffs. Each build ships with documentation and a handover session.

Do I need technical staff?

No. You describe the process and grant access to the relevant tools. We handle the build, testing and documentation.

How is pricing set?

Each engagement is quoted as a fixed scope after a short discovery call. You approve the scope before any build starts.

What happens to our data?

We work with the minimum data needed to test the workflow. We do not use your data to train models, and you can revoke access at any time.

When do you start?

We are opening the first cohort in small batches. Waitlist members are contacted in the order they joined.
