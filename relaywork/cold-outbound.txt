RELAYWORK — COLD OUTBOUND KIT
===============================
Templates only. No contact list is included. Fill in the bracketed fields yourself
from legitimate, public B2B sources, and read the compliance section first.


BEFORE YOU SEND ANYTHING
------------------------
1. Use real identity: your name, your company, a physical postal address, and a
   working reply-to. Never spoof a domain or imply an existing relationship.
2. Every email needs a clear one-click unsubscribe and an honest subject line.
   Honour opt-outs immediately. CAN-SPAM (US), GDPR/PECR (EU/UK), and local
   rules where you operate all apply; this kit is not legal advice.
3. Only contact business addresses in a B2B context. Do not scrape personal
   email addresses, phone numbers, or private profiles. Do not buy lists of
   unknown origin. Prefer a company's published contact page, a public team
   page, or an opt-in industry directory.
4. Cold calling / auto-dialling / recording have their own rules (e.g. TCPA in
   the US). Check local law, do not auto-dial, and never record without consent.
5. Keep volume low and human. Personalise the first line from something actually
   true about their business. Generic blasts burn the domain and the brand.
6. Do not claim customers, results, or numbers you do not have. Relaywork is a
   new business; say so.


WHO TO LOOK FOR (define the segment, then source it legitimately)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Owner / ops lead / finance lead at a 5-50 person agency, e-commerce operator,
  or local service business.
- Trigger signals: hiring for admin roles, "we're swamped" posts, manual
  spreadsheet jobs visible in job ads, repetitive quoting described on their site.
- One named human per company. No catch-all blasts to info@ / sales@.

---------------

posts job ads for a data-entry role twice a year].

Is [SpecificPain] something your team still does by hand? If yes, I can send a

you alone.

[YourName]

PHONE / VOICEMAIL (30 seconds)
------------------------------
"Hi [FirstName], this is [YourName] with Relaywork — I know this is out of the
blue, is now a terrible time? [pause / respect the answer]

[If yes, bad time]: No problem, when's better? Thanks, I'll call then.

[If no]: Thanks. I sent a short note about [SpecificPain]. We're a new studio
that builds AI automations for small teams, and I'm doing a handful of free
20-minute workflow teardowns. No pitch deck — I just want to see whether the
bottleneck is real. Would [Day] at [Time] work?"

Voicemail: "Hi [FirstName], [YourName] at Relaywork, [PhoneNumber]. I sent a
note about [SpecificPain] — no need to call back unless it's useful. Thanks."


REPLY HANDLING
--------------
Interested     -> "Great. Here's a 3-question form (5 min) and two slots. I'll
                  send a fixed scope and price before anything starts."
Not interested -> "Understood, thanks for the straight answer. I won't follow up."
                  Then actually stop and add to your suppression list.
Referral       -> "Thanks — happy to be introduced. I'll only reach out once."
Question about price -> Give a range with the assumptions, not a vague "let's
                  talk". A fixed scope after discovery is the offer.


WHAT TO TRACK (a spreadsheet is enough at this stage)
-----------------------------------------------------
Contacted / Replied / Interested / Teardown booked / Scope sent / Won.
Watch reply rate per 100 sends. If it is under ~3%, the problem is the segment
or the offer — not the copy. Fix that before increasing volume.


WHAT THIS KIT DOES NOT DO
-------------------------
It does not find or harvest anyone's contact details, send emails, place calls,
or book meetings on your behalf. Those steps need your judgement, your sending
domain, and your compliance review.
